# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Brands and sellers on Amazon, Walmart and Shopify, and the teams or agencies running their accounts. They need their accounts watched and fixed without living in dashboards, including outside working hours.

## Product Purpose
Anarix is an e-commerce operations platform. Its AI, **Aan** ("Anarix Analytical Neural"), watches every account around the clock, explains what changed and why, and acts. It catches problems before they cost money and tells the seller what it did. Success means sellers sleep through the 11pm check and wake up to problems already handled.

## Positioning
Aan, the AI inside Anarix, watches every account 24/7 through **Signals** (what moved and why), **Profitability** (true product-level P&L) and **Rules** (plain-language automation that allows good spend and stops waste). It explains each change and acts on it. **Anarix MCP** plugs the same live marketplace data into any AI client: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or a custom agent.

## Operating Context
- The marketing site lives at anarix.ai/platform: Next.js, `basePath /platform`, deployed on Vercel.
- The services site (www.anarix.ai) is a separate project and offers managed account services.
- The product app runs at app.anarix.ai.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Product areas:** Signals, Profitability, Rules / Rule Agents, Ask Aan chat and the morning Brief, MCP, AMC (Amazon Marketing Cloud).
- **Marketplaces:** Amazon, Walmart, Shopify.
- **Terminology:** "Aan" is the AI. It replaces the older name "Jiva"; never use "Jiva" on public surfaces.

## Brand Commitments
- **Aan mascot:** the coral rounded diamond with two dark eyes. State is shape: diamond = idle, circle = listening, bar = working, cube = thinking. It follows the product's AanMascot component exactly, and on the site, docked Aan stays a diamond with eyes on the pointer.
- **Fonts and colors:** the Anarix website system, Fraunces display + Inter body, with periwinkle primary, coral and ink. The user confirmed these are not to change.
- **Voice:** confident, plain and a little playful. Aan is "the guardian of your business". Use the phrase "AI for good".

## Evidence on Hand
- **Proof figures** from the Anarix website hero, approved for public use: $1.2B GMV driven, 500+ brands managed, 12.8% average TACoS.
- **Product UI reference:** the anarix-insight-engine app (Signal cards, Profitability date cards, Applied Rules, Ask Aan Brief).
- **Absent, so never fabricate:** customer names, testimonials, pricing, benchmarks beyond the three figures above. Card numbers on the site are illustrative.

## Product Principles
- Aan acts, then explains: every claim pairs an action with its reason.
- Protect the seller's money and time first; features serve that.
- Same numbers everywhere, across the dashboard, Aan and any AI through MCP.
