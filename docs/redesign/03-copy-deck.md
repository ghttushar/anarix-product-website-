## /platform/signals

### Hero
- **h1:** 3am change. 9am answer.
- **Lede:** Signals watches every price, Buy Box, budget and stock level across Amazon, Walmart and Shopify. When something moves, Aan tells you what changed, why it matters and what to do, before it costs you. *(kept)*

### What it watches
- Price · Buy Box · Ad eligibility · Budget and spend · Stock level, across Amazon, Walmart and Shopify. *(kept)*

### Anatomy of a signal
Callouts on the signal card:
1. **Priority and area:** how urgent, which part of the business, which marketplace.
2. **Money at risk:** the dollar impact, so you know what it's worth before you open it.
3. **What happened, and why:** the change and its root cause in plain words.
4. **Aan's suggested fix:** with Aan's confidence in it.
5. **Act from here:** execute, modify or assign, without opening another tool.

### Ranked by money at risk
- The biggest dollar impact rises to the top, so the first fix is always the one that matters most. *(kept)*

### FAQ
- **What counts as a signal?** A meaningful change on an account (a price, Buy Box, ad eligibility, budget or stock change) together with its root cause and the money at risk.
- **How are signals ranked?** By dollar impact, highest first.
- **Can I hand a signal to my team?** Yes. Assign it from the alert.
- **Does Aan act on signals by itself?** When one of your rules allows it. Otherwise Aan suggests the fix and waits for you. **[review]**
- **Which marketplaces?** Amazon, Walmart and Shopify.
