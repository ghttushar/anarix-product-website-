# 00 · Brief: who this site is for, and what it has to make them believe

**Surface:** anarix.ai/platform, the marketing site for the Anarix platform and its AI, Aan.
**Mode:** Persuade. A visitor succeeds when they understand what Aan is and book a demo or talk to Aan.
**Positioning (owner, 2026-11):** Aan is **your 24/7 e-commerce account manager**. It watches your Amazon, Walmart and Shopify accounts around the clock, explains what changed and why, and acts, so your business grows while you sleep.

---

## 1. The people, in their own scenes

### The brand owner (primary)
Sells on two or three marketplaces and runs a small team or no team at all.
- **Scene.** 11:40 pm, phone in bed, opening Seller Central "one last time". A competitor undercut the price at 9. An ad campaign burned through its budget on a keyword that never converts. The best-seller will stock out on Thursday.
- **Wants.** To stop being the night shift; to know their money is safe; to see where the real profit is.
- **Fears.** Waking up to a loss that happened while they slept. An AI spending their money badly. Yet another dashboard to check.

### The e-commerce manager (decision driver)
In-house and responsible for the numbers across Seller Central, Walmart Seller Center, the Shopify admin and a pile of spreadsheets.
- **Scene.** Monday 9 am. A 40-tab browser, a report due to the founder, and an ad account that changed over the weekend.
- **Wants.** To know what changed and why; to fix it from one place; to look on top of things.
- **Fears.** Missing something that was knowable. Being blamed for a weekend loss. An AI they can't explain to their boss.

### The agency lead (multiplier)
Runs dozens of accounts for clients.
- **Scene.** Thirty client accounts, three account managers, and every client expects an answer by 10 am.
- **Wants.** Leverage; explanations they can forward to a client; their data inside the AI tools they already use.
- **Fears.** Scale breaking quality. Alerts drowning their team.

## 2. Jobs to be done
1. *When I'm not watching,* make sure nothing costly happens to my accounts, so I can sleep and switch off.
2. *When something moves,* tell me what changed, why, and what to do, so I can act in minutes, not after a dig.
3. *When I look at my sales,* show me real profit after fees, ads, refunds and COGS, so I scale what actually makes money.
4. *When I know how I want my accounts run,* let me write it down once and have it enforced every hour, so my playbook runs without me.
5. *When I have a question,* let me ask it plainly and get the answer with the numbers, so I don't have to build a report.
6. *When I already work inside Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini,* let that AI use my live marketplace data, so I don't copy numbers around.

## 3. Objections the site must answer (and where)
| Objection | Answer | Where |

## 4. Belief ladder: one belief per screen, in this order
1. **Hero:** "This is an AI that watches my stores for me."
2. **Mission + proof:** "The people behind it have done this at scale."
3. **The Night Watch:** "It really works while I sleep, and here's what that looks like."
4. **AI for good:** "It is on my side, and I stay in charge."
5. **Signals:** "I will know what changed, why, and what to do."
6. **Profitability:** "I will see real profit, daily."
7. **Rules:** "My playbook will run without me."
8. **Ask Aan:** "I can just ask, and it shows its work."
9. **MCP:** "It works inside the AI tools I already use."
10. **Meet Aan + close:** "I want to meet Aan." → Book a demo / Talk to Aan.

A visitor who leaves at any screen should leave with that screen's belief. That is the test every screen is held to.

## 5. What success looks like
- **Five-second test** on the hero: a first-time visitor can say what it is (an AI account manager), who it's for (Amazon, Walmart and Shopify sellers) and what to do next (book a demo).
- **One-hour memory test:** the visitor describes a coral diamond that watches their stores at night and fixes things before morning, not "a dark website with a lot going on".
- **Behaviour:** more "Book a demo" and "Talk to Aan" actions, and deeper visits to product pages.

## 6. What must stay untouched
- Fraunces and Inter (with IBM Plex Mono for figures), and the periwinkle, coral and ink values. No green anywhere.
- Aan exactly as the product draws it; docked Aan is a diamond.
- The nav bar and the "Ask Aan" island stay at parity with www.anarix.ai.
- The only proof figures are $1.2B GMV, 500+ brands and 12.8% TACoS. There are no customer names, testimonials, prices or invented benchmarks. Numbers inside product UI are sample data and are labelled.

## 7. What would make a polished result feel wrong
- Maximalism: glows, gradients, particles, things moving that explain nothing.
- A data dump: many equal-weight elements with no clear first read.
- Content hanging between screens; long pinned scrolls; anything that hijacks the scroll.
- Cute illustration with no meaning. Every drawing must show how something works.
