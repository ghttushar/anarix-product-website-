# Design System: Anarix Platform

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Long Exposure"**

The platform site is Swiss type shot with the shutter open: periwinkle light, one coral creature, and the real product, moved by the scroll. It now speaks in two registers that share one palette and one creature.

The **studio register** carries the homepage (`/platform`) and Signals (`/platform/signals`). Both are getlayers templates re-purposed for Anarix: the homepage is "AI Studio" (a gradient hero card that tilts and collapses into a 3D ring of the five products over a giant marquee, Aan's white diamond opening into a black world with a particle sphere and the manifesto, pinned horizontal product panels, and fragments drifting past a card that opens into the close), and Signals is "Relay" (black, a halo of light fibres behind everything, dark glass cards, mono labels). Headlines here are Inter 500, larger in size than in weight; light comes from deep-blue-to-periwinkle gradients with a coral glow, from the three.js particle sphere, and from the Signals halo. The homepage is a mix of every product (Signals, Profitability, Rules, MCP, Ask Aan), never a page about Aan alone.

The **screen register** carries the other product pages (Profitability, Rules, MCP with its pinned McpGap scene, and Aan). It is the Phase 8 system: one idea per `Screen`, Inter 800 headlines on a 12-column grid, long-exposure light trails converging on what matters, real product UI on `ProductFrame` cards that turn toward the cursor, and one travelling, limbless Aan.

In both registers the proof is the product: every visual that is not light or Aan is real Anarix UI, and every number in it is labelled as sample data. Aan, the coral diamond from the Ask Aan island, is flat and matte everywhere except one place, the homepage close, where `GlossyAan` renders it as the site's single glossy three.js object. Rejected and gone: soft clay (`ClayBlock`, the clay mascot shading, the clay CTA), the City Nocturne line-art scenes and the soft isometric "circuit" drawings. None of them return.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two registers on one palette: studio (Inter 500, gradient cards, black worlds, pinned GSAP scenes) on the homepage and Signals; screen (Inter 800, `Screen`, `ProductFrame`, `LightTrails`, travelling Aan) on the other product pages.
- Only periwinkle (230) and coral (355) carry hue; studio surfaces blend from one to the other.
- Real product UI everywhere, always labelled "Sample data" or "Illustrative".
- Lenis smooth scroll under GSAP ScrollTrigger timelines that are pinned and scrubbed; transforms and opacity only while scrolling.
- Aan is flat and limbless; the one glossy Aan stands in the homepage close.
- IBM Plex Mono for indexes, clocks, figures and honest labels; Fraunces for written moments and for the single italic word in a Signals headline.

## Colors

Periwinkle is light, action and what Aan handled; coral is Aan or what is going wrong. The screen register takes every role from the `.nocturne` scope (day values under `html[data-theme="light"] .nocturne`); the studio register sets its own fixed grounds and does not change with the theme.

### Primary
- **Working Periwinkle** (`periwinkle-solid`, `--accent-solid`): the fill of the screen register's primary CTA and of solid UI buttons, and the user's question bubble in product UI. Deepens to `periwinkle-solid-hover-night` on hover.
- **Handled Periwinkle** (`periwinkle-night` / `periwinkle-day`, `--accent`): the light itself in the screen register. Light-trail strokes and pools, focus rings, link hover, index numerals, handled states in product UI. Day drops it to 50% lightness to hold contrast on pale sky.
- **Act Periwinkle** (`periwinkle-act`, `--act` on Signals): Signals' action colour, where Relay had lime. Fills the action pill (with Stage Ink text), live dots, progress fills and the halo's dominant fibre colour.
- **Lit** (`lit-night` / `lit-day`, `--lit`): a translucent periwinkle wash for floor glows.

### Secondary
- **Aan Coral** (`aan-coral`, `--risk`, `--coral`): Aan's body, the one risk trail, risk marks in product UI (cost bars, alert dots, a lost Buy Box), and the warm glow at the foot of studio gradients. The mascot's own gradient stops (`#f88a93`, `#f57780`, `#f05e6a`) belong to the mascot and are not tokens.
- **Risk Ink** (`risk-ink-night` / `risk-ink-day`, `--risk-ink`): coral as text, for costs and losses in UI figures.

### Tertiary
- **Studio Gradient** (`studio-deep` → `studio-mid` → `studio-bright`): the homepage's hero card and the Finale card, `linear-gradient(160deg, studio-deep, studio-mid 45%, studio-bright)` under a coral radial glow at the foot and a periwinkle one in the middle. The five product cards and gallery panels each take a variant of it (coral-lit dark for Profitability, deep violet-blue for Rules, near-black with a periwinkle corner for MCP, periwinkle into lilac with a coral foot for Ask Aan).
- **Signals Surface** (`--surface`): `linear-gradient(115deg, #f3828a, #c993d6 48%, #8a9cf6)` under a pale coral radial, the coral to periwinkle surface where Relay used lime.
- **Film Stamp Red** (`film-stamp-red`): the stamped words of the "Aan's debut" film, on its route only.

### Neutral
- **Sky** (`sky-night` / `sky-day`, `--sky`): the screen register's page ground; the body behind `.nocturne` matches it so overscroll never flashes.
- **Ink** and **Ink 2** (`ink-*`, `ink-2-*`): headlines and key phrases in Ink; statements, captions, notes and mono indexes in Ink 2. Signals adds two quieter steps (`--ink-3` `hsl(226 10% 56%)` for mono labels, `--ink-4` `hsl(226 8% 42%)` for dim text).
- **Rule** (`rule-*`, `--rule`): the 1px hairline that groups ruled lists, proof figures and the permission ladder. On black, hairlines are white at 8.5% to 12%.
- **Card** and **Card Line** (`card-*`, `card-line-*`): the ProductFrame and product UI surface and its 1px border. Inside studio cards and panels these roles are re-set locally to translucent navy (`hsl(222 30% 11–12%)` at 70–82%) with a white 10–12% line so the same product UI sits on the gradient.
- **Void** (`void`): the black of the studio register: the world inside Aan's window, the gallery, the Finale, and the whole Signals page.
- **Stage White** and **Stage Ink** (`stage-white`, `stage-ink`): the homepage's white stage behind the product ring and the white frame around the close, with near-black ink for the marquee, the "explore" button and text on light buttons.
- **Bubble** (`bubble-bg-*`, `bubble-ink-*`): Aan's speech bubble, inverted against the theme.
- **Video Ground** (`video-ground`): the near-black behind the film player and product video.
- **Chrome** (`chrome-*`, the `--c-*` set): the nav, Ask Aan island and footer only, ported from www.anarix.ai and switched by `data-nav` / `data-island` / `data-theme`.

### Named Rules
**The No Green Rule.** Positive is periwinkle, negative is coral, in every card, chart, halo and theme. No hue between roughly 60 and 180 enters the site.

**The Two Hues Rule.** Only periwinkle (230) and coral (355) carry hue. A studio surface may blend from one to the other, and the blend's middle stops are the violets between them (`#c993d6`, `#6a5fe0`); a violet never appears on its own as a flat fill, a text colour or a UI state.

**The Coral Is Aan's Rule.** Coral is Aan, something going wrong, or the warm glow at the foot of a studio gradient. It is never a button fill, a heading, a link or a UI state of success.

**The One Risk Trail Rule.** A set of light trails is all periwinkle, except that a screen about a risk may run exactly one trail coral (`risk` index on `LightTrails`), and only while that risk is live.

**The Scope Rule.** In the screen register, colours come from the `.nocturne` roles (`--sky`, `--ink`, `--ink-2`, `--accent`, `--accent-solid`, `--risk`, `--risk-ink`, `--rule`, `--card`, `--card-line`, `--bubble-*`) and theme is the whole page at a different hour. The studio register is the exception and stays fixed in both themes: the homepage is a white stage with black worlds, Signals is black. Studio cards re-set the product UI roles locally so the same components render on them.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Inter (with system-ui, sans-serif), via `--font-display`
**Body Font:** Inter, via `--font-body`
**Label/Mono Font:** IBM Plex Mono (with ui-monospace), via `--font-mono`
**Editorial Font:** Fraunces (with Georgia, serif), via `--font-editorial`

**Character:** Swiss in two weights. The screen register shouts in Inter 800 with tight tracking; the studio register speaks in Inter 500, large and tight, with a second line or phrase dropped to white at 55–60%. Plex Mono does the counting and labelling in small sizes; Fraunces appears where a piece of writing should feel written, and as one italic word in a Signals headline.

### Hierarchy
- **Display Name** (Inter 800, 0.9, -0.055em): a product's own name in its `NameHero`, the largest type on its page. Size is set per hero layout, from `clamp(3.5rem, min(9vw, 13svh), 9rem)` up to `clamp(5rem, min(14vw, 24svh), 15rem)`.
- **Headline** (Inter 800, `--fs-h2`, 0.98, -0.04em, balanced): every screen heading in the screen register (`.t-h2`).
- **Studio Display** (Inter 500, 1.02, -0.045em): the Signals hero headline. The homepage hero title (`clamp(2.3rem, 3.7vw, 4.2rem)`, 1, -0.04em) and the Finale close (`clamp(2.4rem, 4.6vw, 5rem)`) are the same face and weight at their own sizes, each with its second line at white 55–60%.
- **Studio Headline** (Inter 500, 1.08, -0.04em): Signals section headings.
- **Marquee** (Inter 500, `clamp(3.4rem, 7.2vw, 8.5rem)`, -0.045em, Stage Ink): the giant words that slide behind the product ring, separated by small outlined circles. The gallery's product names use the same weight at `clamp(2.6rem, 5.2vw, 5.6rem)` in white.
- **Slogan** (Inter 600, 1.1, -0.015em, Ink 2): the line under a product name.
- **Figure** (Inter 800, 1, -0.04em, tabular): proof figures in a ruled row. Signals' figures are Inter 500, tabular, `clamp(2.8rem, 4.4vw, 3.8rem)`, counted up once.
- **Editorial** (Fraunces 400, 1.5rem, 1.1, -0.01em): the morning brief title and the letter on the Aan page. In Signals, Fraunces 400 italic (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) sets one word or phrase inside an Inter 500 headline.
- **Statement** (Inter 400, `--fs-statement`, 1.5, max 42 to 44ch, Ink 2): the paragraph under a screen-register headline, key phrases in `<b>` at 500 and full Ink. Studio ledes are 0.875–0.9375rem at 1.55–1.6, white at 80% or Ink 2.
- **Point title / text** (Inter 600 15px / 400 14px, 1.45, Ink 2): ruled points. Descriptions hide below 800px of viewport height.
- **Index** (Plex Mono 500, 0.75rem, 0.06em, Ink 2): sequence numerals ("01 / 05" on the ring cards, "01" beside a gallery name, ladder rungs).
- **Frame note** (Plex Mono 500, 0.6875rem): the honest label in a ProductFrame's pill, a gallery panel's corner and Signals' mono labels (`0.6875rem/1.4`, `--ink-3`).

### Named Rules
**The Weight Per Register Rule.** Screen-register headlines are Inter 800 between -0.04em and -0.055em, line-height under 1, fluid by both width and height (`min(vw, svh)`). Studio-register headlines are Inter 500 between -0.04em and -0.045em, line-height 1 to 1.08. Never mix the two weights in one section.

**The Grey Prose, Ink Phrase Rule.** Prose is the quieter ink; only the phrases that carry the argument switch to full ink at 500. In studio headlines the same move runs the other way: the second line drops to white at 55–60%. Emphasis is contrast, not colour or size.

**The Written Word Rule.** Fraunces is for writing that reads as written by someone (the morning brief, Aan's letter) and, in Signals only, for one italic word per headline. It never sets a whole headline, a figure or UI.

**The Mono Counts Rule.** Plex Mono sets numbers that count, time or order something, and the sample-data notes. Every figure that changes uses tabular numerals.

## Layout

**Screen register.** One idea, one screen. A `Screen` is a grid of three rows: `--inset-top` (84px) for the nav, the content (`--content-h`), and `--inset-bottom` (90px) for the island. `fit="screen"` holds exactly one viewport; `fit="grow"` starts at one viewport and may run taller when the information needs it. Below 620px of viewport height any screen may grow rather than clip. Content sits in `.l-container`, `min(1280px, 100% - 48px)` (32px gutter below 400px), on `.l-grid`: 12 columns with a 24px gap. Product screens alternate a 5fr / 7fr split and collapse to one column at 900px.

**Studio register.** The homepage is three pinned scenes, each exactly one viewport (`100svh`, overflow hidden) and each a GSAP ScrollTrigger timeline with `pin: true` and `scrub: 1`: `Intro` (pinned for 720% of a viewport), `Gallery` (pinned for the track's overflow width) and `Finale` (360%). Copy sits at the studio inset (`clamp(20px, 3.4vw, 56px)`) from the frame's corners: title top left, lede, buttons and proof pills bottom left, a two-line corner note bottom right. Product cards are `clamp(236px, 21.5vw, 318px)` wide at a 1:1.46 ratio, on a ring of radius 0.86 card widths. Signals flows instead of pinning: sections in `min(1180px, 100% - 2 × clamp(20px, 5vw, 64px))`, a hero of at least one viewport on a 0.92fr / 1.08fr split, and one pinned scene (the panel that grows out of a tile, 240%).

Spacing is a 4px scale (`--s-1` 4px to `--s-9` 96px) plus three height-aware gaps (`--gap-s`, `--gap-m`, `--gap-l`). Breakpoints in use: 1100px, 900px (splits stack), 760px (cards go flat; studio cards shrink to 56vw and the corner note hides), 640px, 400px; heights 800px, 760px, 740px (short phones hide the hero card) and 620px.

### Named Rules
**The One Viewport Rule.** Above 620px of height, a `fit="screen"` section and every pinned studio scene is exactly one viewport; cut copy or hide optional detail instead. QA is `scripts/qa/screens.mjs` at 1280x720, 1366x768, 1440x900, 1920x1080 and 390x844 with `fit-check.js`: a `Screen` is checked at its top, a `StoryScroll` at the middle of each chapter, and a GSAP-pinned `[data-pin]` scene at its start and at 36%, 68% and 98% through its pin-spacer. Flowing Relay sections on Signals are not screens and are not held to one viewport.

**The Pinned Scene Rule.** A long story is a pinned, scrubbed scene, and its number is set by the page's register: the homepage is three pinned scenes back to back, Signals has one, a screen-register page has at most one (`StoryScroll` or a GSAP pin, such as MCP's `McpGap`).

**The Ruled, Not Boxed Rule.** In the screen register, group with space first and a 1px Rule hairline second (`.c-points`, proof figures, the permission ladder). Only product UI, studio cards, the film and video frames are boxed, and nothing is a card inside a card except product UI inside a studio card or panel, which is the template's own device.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is light and space, not material. The screen register stays flat on Sky; only `ProductFrame` cards stand off it, in perspective (1600px, origin 50% 30%) with a base tilt of `rotateY(-4deg) rotateX(2deg)` (mirrored for `lean="right"`) and a pointer that adds up to 2.5deg, an oval periwinkle floor glow beneath and a 420px cursor spotlight above. The studio register builds real 3D with CSS transforms: the hero tilts in a 1400px perspective, the five product cards stand on a `preserve-3d` ring in a 1500px stage, and the window out of Aan's shape scales past the screen's edges. Light in the studio register is the three.js particle sphere, the Signals halo (fibres with UnrealBloom and a white-hot core) and soft radial glows; studio cards and glass panels carry long, deep drop shadows.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Frame drop** (`0 1px 0 hsl(0 0% 100% / 0.06) inset, 0 50px 90px -40px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.65), 0 18px 36px -24px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.5)`): ProductFrame at night. Day uses `0 50px 80px -40px hsl(230 40% 30% / 0.35), 0 18px 36px -24px hsl(230 40% 30% / 0.25)`.
- **UI card drop** (`0 24px 48px -32px hsl(228 60% 4% / 0.7)`): a product UI card shown without a frame.
- **Studio card drop** (`0 40px 70px -40px rgb(10 14 40 / 0.55)`): a product card on the white stage.
- **Glass drop** (`0 50px 90px -40px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.8)` to `0 50px 140px -50px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.95)`): product UI floating on a gallery panel and Signals' dark glass cards.
- **Act glow** (`0 0 28px hsl(230 85% 75% / 0.45)`): the Signals action pill on hover; live dots carry an 8px glow of their own colour.
- **Bubble** (`0 8px 20px -12px hsl(228 50% 4% / 0.6)`): Aan's speech bubble.
- **Chrome soft / medium / strong** (`--c-shadow-*`): nav and island only.

### Named Rules
**The Light Is The Depth Rule.** Lift comes from light (trails, pools, floor glows, the cursor spotlight, the particle sphere, the halo, radial glows) and from real perspective, never from a hover lift or a hard offset shadow. Every surface is matte. The one glossy object on the site is `GlossyAan` in the homepage close (a clearcoated coral `MeshPhysicalMaterial` lit by a studio environment), standing where AI Studio has its chrome star; nothing else gets a sheen or a glossy gradient.

**The No Clay Rule.** Soft clay is retired. Aan, the primary CTA and every block are flat: no lit top edges, core shadows, contact shadows or pressed-in labels.

## Shapes

Soft rectangles and pills. Product UI cards round at 20px, ProductFrame and Signals glass cards at 16px, Aan's bubble at 14px with the corner nearest Aan cut to 4px, buttons inside UI at 10px, chart bars at 4px. Studio shapes: the hero and Finale frames at 28px, inset 12px from the viewport; the ring's product cards at 22px; gallery panels at 6px; studio buttons on the homepage at 10px. Every screen-register CTA, every Signals button, pill, chip and frame note is a full pill (999px). Borders are 1px hairlines. Aan is the one organic form: a 45deg diamond with a corner radius of 17% of its size (22% on the ring card's white diamond, and the window that grows out of it), which morphs into a cube (thinking) and a bar (working).

### Motion
**Smooth scroll.** `SmoothScroll` (in the site layout) runs Lenis (`lerp 0.085`, touch multiplier 1.4) from the GSAP ticker with lag smoothing off, and ScrollTrigger updates from Lenis, so scrubbed timelines read one smooth value per frame. Hash links glide via Lenis. Reduced motion keeps the browser's own scrolling.

**Scroll scenes.** Each scene is one GSAP timeline in `gsap.matchMedia`, pinned and scrubbed (`scrub: 1`, `invalidateOnRefresh`, `anticipatePin`), with GSAP owning every transform it moves, centring included. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the scene sets `data-static="true"` and shows its end state laid out in flow: the hero, the five cards and the manifesto one after another; the gallery row scrolls sideways by hand; the close framed in white without fragments.

**Curves and durations.** `--ease-arrive` `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for what arrives or settles; `--ease-travel` `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` for Aan's flights and trail pulses; `--ease-spring` `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` for Aan's acts; Signals uses `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` and GSAP's `power` eases. Durations: micro 160ms, UI 320ms, reveal 700ms, flight 600ms, stagger 90ms. `Reveal` is the blur-rise (opacity 0, 16px down, 4px blur) and runs only when script is running.

**The Smooth Scroll Rule.** Nothing re-renders React per scroll frame: scroll scenes write transforms and opacity through GSAP, the halo sets shader uniforms, and layout is measured on ScrollTrigger refresh only (`invalidateOnRefresh`, function-based values, the halo's `measure` on `refresh`). WebGL draws only while it can be seen (the sphere is switched by its ScrollTrigger, Glossy Aan by an IntersectionObserver, the halo stops when the tab is hidden). Reduced motion shows each scene's end state and leaves Lenis off.

**The Transform And Opacity Rule.** Animate transform and opacity. The exceptions the build carries are the blur-rise reveals, the trails' stroke draw, colour and glow transitions on hover and state, Aan's cube and bar morphs, and the Intro's hero frame collapsing onto its centre card by `clip-path`; add no more.

**The Depth Drift Rule.** Parallax exists only inside pinned studio scenes, as depth: each gallery panel's UI drifts 14% against its frame, and the Finale's fragments travel at speeds set by their depth. The screen register has none.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep periwinkle and coral as the only hues; let studio gradients blend between them and nowhere else.
- **Do** take every screen-register colour from the `.nocturne` roles so night and day both work, and keep the studio register's fixed grounds (white stage and black worlds on the homepage, black on Signals).
- **Do** set screen-register headlines in Inter 800 (`.t-h2`, the name sizes) and studio headlines in Inter 500, and keep Fraunces for the morning brief, Aan's letter and one italic word per Signals headline.
- **Do** show real product UI with an honest label ("Sample data", "Sample rule", "Sample conversation", "Sample message", "Illustrative"), in a `ProductFrame` or on a studio card or panel.
- **Do** give the homepage every product (Signals, Profitability, Rules, MCP, Ask Aan), never a page about Aan alone.
- **Do** keep proof figures to $1.2B GMV, 500+ brands and 12.8% TACoS.
- **Do** name marketplaces and apps (Amazon, Walmart, Shopify, Slack, Claude) as text.
- **Do** build scroll scenes as pinned, scrubbed GSAP timelines over Lenis, moving transforms and opacity, measuring on refresh only, and mark them `data-pin` so `fit-check.js` checks them through the pin.
- **Do** pause WebGL when it cannot be seen and load three.js only when the component mounts.
- **Do** design the reduced-motion state on purpose: each scene's end state shown in flow, nothing travelling, no Lenis.
- **Do** keep the nav, the Ask Aan island and the footer at parity with www.anarix.ai.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use green, teal or any hue outside periwinkle (230) and coral (355), or a flat violet fill on its own.
- **Don't** use coral for buttons, headings, links or success states.
- **Don't** give Aan arms, legs, hands or a face beyond its two eyes, or restyle it outside the island's `AanMascot`, the ring card's white diamond and `GlossyAan`.
- **Don't** bring back soft clay, City Nocturne line-art scenes or the soft isometric drawings.
- **Don't** add a second glossy object; `GlossyAan` in the homepage close is the only one.
- **Don't** set React state from scroll, animate layout properties while scrolling, or measure the DOM on a scroll frame.
- **Don't** show an unlabelled number in product UI, or invent customers, testimonials, logos or prices.
- **Don't** draw marketplace or app logos.
- **Don't** let a `fit="screen"` section or a pinned scene grow past one viewport above 620px of height.
- **Don't** add hover lifts, hard offset shadows or gradient text.
- **Don't** add parallax outside a pinned studio scene, or a second pinned story to a screen-register page.
- **Don't** bring the film's stamp red, Bodoni or grain onto site pages.
