# Design System: Anarix Platform

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "City Nocturne"**

The platform site is one slate night, the same `#0e151b` as the services site (www.anarix.ai), and on it the platform is drawn as Soft Isometric Systems: small machines of tonal blocks in a 2:1 isometric projection, each edged in one hairline, each a labelled system with named inputs, the Anarix core where Aan docks, and a named outcome. The whole site sits inside one `.nocturne` scope: in the dark theme (the default, set on `<html data-theme="dark">` before first paint) the faces are steps of the night; in the light theme the same machines are white and pale periwinkle on a pale sky. Every screen is the same sky, the same ink, the same face tones. The earlier hand-drawn street of storefronts was retired as too childish; only the MCP wiring map, tool panel and pole remain in 2D line art, because they are diagrams of wiring and read best flat.

Pages are made of screens, and a screen holds one idea: a Fraunces headline, one statement in grey Inter with its key phrases lifted to full ink, then either an isometric system that explains a mechanism or the real product card proving it, and a few ruled points. Information sections (How it works, What it does, Works with, the product tour) may grow taller than one viewport; everything else is one screen. Motion has one job, cause and effect: a stage sits quiet until its screen arrives, its blocks settle in one after another, Aan docks on the core, and then the stage powers on: marked faces lift toward periwinkle (what Aan handled) or coral (what was going wrong), and one slow packet runs each cable. Nothing glows or decorates.

Two pieces of chrome are not part of the world and do not follow its rules: the nav and the Ask Aan action island are a 1:1 port of the services site (www.anarix.ai) and stay at parity with it, including their pill shapes, glass, soft shadows and lift-on-hover buttons.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One `.nocturne` world in two lights: night (dark, default) and day (light). Theme is a whole-page switch saved in `localStorage` (`anarix-theme-v2`), never a lit panel inside a dark page.
- Two hues only, periwinkle (230) and coral (355), over slate and navy neutrals. No green anywhere.
- Fraunces 600 speaks, Inter explains, IBM Plex Mono measures (figures only).
- Soft Isometric Systems: tonal blocks in a 2:1 projection, one 1px hairline edge, labels as live type; colour on a face means something (periwinkle data and outcome, coral risk and cost).
- One idea per screen between an 84px nav inset and a 90px island inset; information sections may grow.
- Proximity and hairline rules group content; the only boxes are product UI cards, the video frame and the callout cards pinned to drawings.
- Aan is the unchanged brand mascot, docks as a diamond on the core of each system, and is the warmest object on every screen.

## Colors

A slate night matched to the services site (or a pale periwinkle day) inked in slate, with one periwinkle working accent and one coral that belongs to Aan and to risk.

### Primary
- **Working Periwinkle** (`--accent-solid`, periwinkle-solid): the filled primary button in both themes, the primary button inside product cards, the Night Watch scrubber fill, the "you" bubble in the Ask Aan chat and the visitor's question on the homepage Ask Aan screen. Its hover deepens (periwinkle-solid-hover-night in the dark theme, periwinkle-day in the light).
- **Handled Periwinkle** (`--accent`, periwinkle-night in dark, periwinkle-day in light): the readable periwinkle. It is what an isometric face lifts toward when Aan has handled it (mixed 16% to 36% into the face tone), the packet on a cable, a hot tag and its pin, positive figures in product cards (`gain`), step numbers, hover color on text links and FAQ summaries, focus rings inside the world, and the third line of the hero headline.
- **Chrome Periwinkle** (periwinkle-chrome-night in dark, periwinkle-solid in light): the services-site primary used by the nav and island chrome and by the global `:focus-visible` ring and `::selection` (18% alpha).
- **Lit** (`--lit`, lit-night / lit-day): the Night Watch dawn that lifts the sky in the last hour.

### Secondary
- **Aan Coral** (`--risk`, aan-coral, the same in both themes): Aan's body, and in drawings the tone a face lifts toward when it is the problem (the moved price tile, the cost lifted out of the sales column, the change a rule stops), the packet on a risk cable and the pin of a risk callout. aan-coral-light and aan-coral-deep exist only inside the mascot's body gradient (`AanMascot.tsx`).
- **Risk Ink** (`--risk-ink`, risk-ink-night / risk-ink-day): coral text that must be read, such as costs and money at risk in product cards, risk tags and callout figures on drawings. It is lighter at night and deeper by day so it holds contrast.

### Tertiary
- **Film Stamp Red** (film-stamp-red): only on the `/film/debut` route, for the Bodoni stamps. It never appears on site pages.

### Neutral
- **Night Sky / Day Sky** (`--sky`, sky-night / sky-day): the ground of every page. The browser theme color matches (`#0e151b` dark, `#f3f4fc` light).
- **Isometric faces** (iso-top, iso-left, iso-right, with a darker plate set for the base plates and quiet parts, each with a night and day value): three tonal steps of the night per block, top lightest and right face darkest, so form reads without a light source or gradient. Tones on top of them are mixes, not new colours: `data` (the Anarix data layer, 5% to 9% periwinkle), `glass` (periwinkle at 4% to 10% over transparent), `hot` and `risk` (16% to 38% toward periwinkle or coral, edges at 55%).
- **Iso edge / Iso wire** (iso-edge, iso-wire): the hairline that edges every face, engraved plate grids and tag leaders; the slightly stronger cable at rest. Both tint to 60% periwinkle or coral when the stage powers on.
- **Line / Line Strong** (`--line`, `--line-strong`): the ink of the remaining 2D line art (MCP wiring map, tool panel, pole).
- **Ink / Ink 2** (`--ink`, `--ink-2`): primary text and tag labels; the grey of statements, ruled-point descriptions, tag sub-lines, captions and the footer.
- **Rule** (`--rule`): every hairline between list rows, the proof row, the footer and the film player border.
- **Card / Card Line** (`--card`, `--card-line`): product UI card and callout card surface, the video frame border and its fallback ground (card at 72%), chips and inner rules.
- **Video ground** (video-ground): the near-black behind a real recording in the video frame.
- **Bubble** (`--bubble-bg`, `--bubble-ink`): Aan's speech bubble inverts the theme, pale at night and slate ink by day. The "Watch with sound" pill uses the pale night pair in both themes.
- **Chrome neutrals** (chrome-card, chrome-fg, chrome-muted-fg, chrome-muted, chrome-border, chrome-accent, each with a day and night value): the services-site chrome palette, used only by the nav, the island and the Ask Aan panel.

### Named Rules
**The No Green Rule.** Positive is periwinkle, negative is coral, in every card, drawing and theme. No hue between roughly 60 and 180 enters the site.

**The Coral Is Aan's Rule.** Coral is Aan, or it is something going wrong. It is never a button, a heading, a link or decoration, and Aan stays the warmest, most saturated object on any screen.

**The One Street Rule.** Theme is the whole world at a different hour. Colors change only through the `.nocturne` scope (dark values, and `html[data-theme="light"] .nocturne`, or `html[data-theme="light"] .stage` for isometric faces); no section re-scopes itself into the other theme.

**The Meaningful Face Rule.** An isometric face is neutral night unless it carries meaning: periwinkle for data, Anarix and what Aan handled, coral for risk and cost. Colour is never used to make a block prettier.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Fraunces (600; 400 and 700 also loaded; fallback Georgia, serif)
**Body Font:** Inter (400, 500, 600, 700; fallback system-ui, sans-serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** IBM Plex Mono (400, 500; fallback ui-monospace, monospace), for figures only

**Character:** A soft, high-contrast serif carries every claim at poster scale; a neutral grotesk carries everything a person has to read. Mono appears only where something is counted or timed.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Fraunces 600, fluid by width and height and capped at 96px, line-height 1.02): the homepage hero h1, set as three short lines, the last in Handled Periwinkle. Product page closing titles use the same voice capped at 88px (`clamp(2.5rem, min(5.4vw, 9svh), 5.5rem)`).
- **Display Name** (Fraunces 600, line-height 0.92, -0.035em): the product's name in its hero, the largest type on the page. Each hero composition sets its own clamp, from `clamp(3.5rem, min(9vw, 13svh), 9rem)` (ledger) to `clamp(5rem, min(14vw, 24svh), 15rem)` (portrait); the frontmatter value is the split hero's.
- **Slogan** (Fraunces 600, 22px to 36px fluid, line-height 1.1, Ink 2): the line directly under a product name.
- **Headline** (Fraunces 600, capped at 64px, line-height 1.04, balanced): one per screen, at most two short lines, max 18ch on information screens. The homepage mission line and proof figures are set in Fraunces 600 between Headline and Statement size.
- **Step title** (Fraunces 600, 20px to 26px, line-height 1.15): the title of each How it works step, over a 16px / 1.55 Ink 2 line at 34ch. Tour chapter names use the same voice at 16px to 20px.
- **Statement** (Inter 400, 17px to 22px fluid, line-height 1.5, max 42ch; 52ch on information screens): the one paragraph under each headline, in Ink 2, with its key phrases in full Ink at 500 weight.
- **Capability title / text** (Inter 600 17px, line-height 1.3; Inter 400 15.5px, line-height 1.55, Ink 2): the cells of What it does.
- **Body** (Inter 400, 16px, line-height 1.6): running text, FAQ answers at 14.5px / 1.55.
- **Screen point title / text** (Inter 600 15.5px; Inter 400 14.5px, line-height 1.5, Ink 2): the three points on each homepage product screen.
- **Point title / Point text** (Inter 600 15px; Inter 400 14px, line-height 1.45, Ink 2): the two lines of a ruled point elsewhere.
- **CTA** (Inter 600, 15px): filled buttons and text links.
- **Chip** (Inter 400, 13.5px, Ink): the names in a Works-with group.
- **Label** (Inter 600, 13px, line-height 1.3): Aan's speech bubble, footer column heads, Works-with group names (in Ink 2), UI buttons and callout card titles.
- **Drawing label** (Inter 600, 13px, 0.01em, Ink or the tag's tone, sub-line 400 at 86% size in Ink 2): a Tag on an isometric drawing. **Face text** (Inter 600, 14px default, 0.02em, Ink at 88%; Plex Mono 500 for figures) is type printed on a block's face. Both are live SVG text, never paths, and never below 11px on screen at any stage size.
- **UI label / Caption** (Inter 600 / 400, 12px): pills, field labels, callout card figures; the "Sample data" note under each card or drawing. The video frame's "coming soon" chip is 500 12.5px.
- **UI figure** (Inter 700, 28px to 36px, -0.02em, tabular): the headline number inside a product card.
- **Figure** (IBM Plex Mono 500, 12px to 13px, tabular): times in the Night Watch log, card metadata, step and chapter numbers (`01`, `02`), anatomy callout numbers.

### Named Rules
**The Grey Prose, Ink Phrase Rule.** Statements are Ink 2; only the phrases that carry the argument switch to full Ink. Emphasis is contrast, not color or size.

**The Mono Measures Rule.** IBM Plex Mono is for numbers that count or time something, always with tabular numerals. It is never used for labels, headings or UI text.

**The Fraunces Ceiling Rule.** Display type is fluid by both width and height (`min(vw, svh)`). The homepage display never exceeds 96px and headlines never exceed 64px; only a product's own name in its hero may go larger. Type shrinks on short screens before a screen is allowed to grow.

## Layout

The page is a stack of screens. A screen (`Screen`) is split into three rows: the nav inset (84px), the content, and the island inset (90px). Content lives in a 1280px measure with 24px gutters (16px below 400px wide) on a 12-column grid with a 24px column gap.

A screen has one of two fits. `fit="screen"` (the default) is exactly one viewport (`min-height: 100svh`); below 620px of height it may grow, anywhere else growth is a bug fixed by editing copy. `fit="grow"` is at least one viewport and runs taller when its information needs the room, with its content centred; it is used for information sections only: the product tour, the homepage product screens, Works with, How it works, What it does and the product close with its questions. QA (`scripts/qa/fit-check.js`) skips the height check for grow screens, and for a grow screen that is actually taller than the viewport it checks clearance against the nav only, since such a section scrolls under the island by design.

**Homepage.** Twelve screens in fixed order: hero (three-line display and actions left, the PlatformStack right), mission with proof, Product tour (the 16:9 video frame across 8 columns, heading and line in the remaining 4), Night Watch (NightBoard), AI for good (Guardrails), five product screens (Signals, Profitability, Rules, Ask Aan, MCP), Works with (three ruled columns of names: marketplaces, team apps, AI), and Meet Aan with the film. Each product screen pairs a hook headline, statement, three points and a link (5 fractions) with a preview of its page (7 fractions), and the copy side alternates left, right, left, right, left so the five read as a sequence; previews are SignalsBoard, ProfitStack, RuleGate, a conversation answered by the docked Aan, and the MCP pole over the terminal, each with a "Sample …" caption.

**Product pages** (`/platform/[product]`) run: name hero with the video frame (MCP keeps its wiring-map hero), How it works, the hands-on moment, What it does with Works-with groups, proof, and close. How it works has three variants: **row** (numbered columns, each opened by a hairline), **rail** (heading sticky on the left, steps down the right with large Fraunces numbers) and **aside** (heading across, steps down the left, a second view of the system on the right). What it does has two: **grid** (ruled cells in three columns, two from 901px to 1100px) and **list** (intro left, two ruled columns right).

**Density tiers.** Spacing is the 4px scale (4 to 96px) plus three fluid gaps that tighten on short screens first. Below 800px of height, optional items (`data-optional`) hide; below 760px gaps and the mission line tighten further. Below 900px wide everything stacks to one column, alternating product screens put copy first, and variant grids collapse to one column. Below 640px the MCP pole on its product screen and other optional drawings hide. Isometric stages also measure themselves (see Components).

Breakpoints: 400px, 640px, 900px, 1100px wide (1100px is also the chrome's desktop menu); 620px, 760px, 800px tall. Stage container queries: 340px, 380px, 520px, 640px.

## Elevation & Depth

The world is flat light, not flat form. Isometric blocks carry depth through three tonal faces per block (top lightest, right darkest) and draw order, never through gradients, highlights or blur. Each stage stands on one soft ground shadow, an isometric ellipse filled with iso-shadow. The other shadows inside the world are two soft, dark, downward drops: one under product UI cards so they read as real objects, and one under Aan's speech bubble. Glass (backdrop blur) and the periwinkle-tinted soft/medium/strong shadow scale belong to the chrome alone.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Stage ground** (`fill: hsl(212 40% 2% / 0.32)` at night, `hsl(228 40% 40% / 0.12)` by day, an isometric ellipse): the one shadow under an isometric system.
- **Card drop** (`box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -32px hsl(228 60% 4% / 0.7)`): every product UI card.
- **Bubble drop** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -12px hsl(228 50% 4% / 0.6)`): Aan's speech bubble.
- **Chrome soft / medium / strong** (`0 2px 8px -2px`, `0 4px 16px -4px`, `0 8px 32px -8px` in periwinkle at 0.08 / 0.12 / 0.18 alpha by day, black at 0.3 / 0.4 / 0.5 by night): the scrolled nav, chrome buttons and the Ask Aan panel, at services-site parity.

### Named Rules
**The Tonal Faces Rule.** Form comes from three flat face tones and a hairline edge. Nothing in the world glows, shines or blurs; a handled state is a face lifting toward periwinkle, never a halo. The one soft light is the Night Watch dawn, a `--lit` wash rising from the bottom of the sky in the last hour of the night, because the screen is about the night ending.

## Shapes

Isometric systems are built from a small kit: boxes (`Block`), short upright cylinders (`Cyl`, Aan's plinth, sockets), rings lying on a surface, engraved plate grids, cables routed along the world axes with round caps and joins, and pinned tags. Edges are one 1px hairline at any stage size (cables 1.25px, the packet 3.2px). The remaining 2D line art (MCP) is monoline at 1.4px with round caps and joins.

UI follows the product: product cards at 20px, the video frame at 18px, buttons inside cards and callout cards on drawings at 10px, pills and chips fully round (999px), the speech bubble at 14px with a 4px corner on the side its tail points from. Callout pins are 7px dots; tag pins are 2.6-unit dots. Callout numbers are 22px circles. Focus rings are 2px periwinkle outlines with an 8px radius globally (6px on list rows and FAQ summaries, 4px in the footer). Aan's own geometry is the brand exception: a square rotated 45 degrees with an 18% corner radius.

**The Name First Rule.** A product page never opens on a slogan alone; its name is the largest type on the page.

**The One Moment Rule.** One hands-on moment per page, unique to it, built from real buttons or tabs, starting in a state that already makes the point.

**The Labelled System Rule.** Every drawing is a labelled system: its inputs and outcome are named in live type (tags, face text or callouts), Aan docks on the core, and its `aria-label` says in a sentence what the system does. A drawing that only decorates does not ship.

**The Words Step Aside Rule.** On small stages the words step aside rather than crowd the drawing: stages are size containers, tags and column labels hide below 520px of stage width (column labels below 640px), callouts below 380px and face text below 340px. The drawing still reads as a system; the copy beside it carries the meaning.

**The Frame Before Recordings Rule.** The video frame ships before the recording exists. Until then it shows the page's own system (or the tour chapters) inside the same 16:9 frame with a "… coming soon" chip, so the space is reserved and nothing looks broken; it never shows an empty player or a fake play button over nothing.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep Fraunces + Inter and the periwinkle, coral and ink tokens exactly as defined in `app/styles/tokens.css`; use IBM Plex Mono only for figures.
- **Do** put every page inside the `.nocturne` scope and take colors from its tokens (`--sky`, `--ink`, `--accent`, `--risk`, and the stage's `--iso-*` faces), so both night and day work.
- **Do** build each section as one `Screen`; use `fit="grow"` only for information sections (tour, product screens, How it works, What it does, Works with, close with questions).
- **Do** group with space first and a 1px Rule hairline second; box only product UI, the video frame and drawing callouts.
- **Do** caption every product card or preview "Sample data" (or "Sample conversation", "Sample rule", "Sample session", "Illustrative") and label any illustrative number as such.
- **Do** limit proof figures to $1.2B GMV, 500+ brands and 12.8% TACoS, set in a ruled row.
- **Do** draw new illustrations with the isometric kit (`IsoStage`, `Block`, `Cable`, `Tag`, `FaceText`, `Callout`) on `lib/iso.ts`: tonal faces, a 1px edge, words as live type, coral for what goes wrong and periwinkle for data and what Aan handled.
- **Do** make every drawing a labelled system with named inputs, a core where Aan docks and a named outcome, and give it a one-sentence `aria-label`.
- **Do** reserve an `AanDock` on the system's core, with a bubble of 28 characters or fewer, and dock Aan as the diamond.
- **Do** put a product's tour in `ProductVideo` (16:9, 18px radius) and show the page's system with a "coming soon" chip until the recording exists.
- **Do** design the reduced-motion state on purpose: final states shown, packets stopped, 200ms fades kept.
- **Do** keep the nav and Ask Aan island at parity with www.anarix.ai, including the transparent-until-scroll nav.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use green, teal or any hue outside periwinkle (230) and coral (355) for status, charts, faces or decoration.
- **Don't** put a label, eyebrow, kicker or numbered pill above a headline; the headline carries its own weight. The one exception: each homepage product screen sets the product's name (13px, periwinkle) above its hook, because the hook ("3am change. 9am answer.") does not name the product and the owner asked to always know which product is which.
- **Don't** use gradient text, gradient faces, glows, halos, shine sweeps or hover lifts on the site; the chrome's glass and lift are services-site parity, not a pattern to copy.
- **Don't** bring back the City Nocturne scene drawings (street, shopfronts, night town, home at night, window close-up, ledger, gatehouse, portrait) or draw new scenes in 2D line art; line art is reserved for the MCP wiring map, tool panel and pole.
- **Don't** set figures as big-number tiles or hero-metric templates; figures live in ruled rows, product cards or drawing callouts.
- **Don't** lay content out as grids of boxed tiles where a ruled list will do.
- **Don't** let a `fit="screen"` screen grow past one viewport above 620px of height; cut copy or hide `data-optional` items instead.
- **Don't** redraw, recolor or restyle Aan, dock it on product UI, or show it docked as circle, bar or cube.
- **Don't** add idle loops beyond the one slow packet per powered cable, parallax, scroll pinning or scroll-scrubbing; motion follows cause and effect.
- **Don't** fabricate customers, testimonials, logos or prices, or show an unlabelled number in product UI; Works-with names are type, never logos.
- **Don't** use coral for buttons, headings, links or decoration.
- **Don't** bring the film's stamp red, Bodoni or grain onto site pages.
