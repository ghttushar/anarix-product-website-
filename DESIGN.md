# Design System: Anarix Platform

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Long Exposure"**

The platform site is Swiss type shot with the shutter open: periwinkle light, one coral creature, and the real app, moved by the scroll. It speaks in two registers that share one palette and one creature, and every page is built from three colour layers: the page ground, brand stages and product mockups.

The **studio register** carries the homepage (`/platform`) and Signals (`/platform/signals`). Both are getlayers templates re-purposed for Anarix. The homepage is "AI Studio": a gradient hero card with real app pieces floating in it, which tilts and closes onto the first card of a 3D ring of six products over a giant marquee; Aan's white diamond opening into a black world with a particle sphere and "AI for good"; pinned horizontal story panels; and app fragments drifting past a card that opens into the close. Signals is "Relay": black, a halo of light fibres behind everything, dark glass cards, mono labels, and the real Signals UI. Headlines here are Inter 500, larger in size than in weight; light comes from deep-blue-to-periwinkle gradients with a coral glow, from the three.js particle sphere and from the Signals halo. The homepage covers the whole platform (Signals, Profitability, Advertising, Rules, MCP, Ask Aan), never Aan alone.

The **screen register** carries the other product pages (Profitability, Rules, MCP with its pinned McpGap scene, and Aan). It is the Phase 8 system: one idea per `Screen`, Inter 800 headlines on a 12-column grid, long-exposure light trails converging on what matters, product UI on `ProductFrame` cards that turn toward the cursor, and one travelling, limbless Aan.

In both registers the proof is the product. On the homepage and Signals it is the app UI kit (`components/app-ui`): close recreations of single elements of the real Anarix app, in the app's own colours, following the theme the way the app does. Every number in it is labelled as sample data. Aan, the coral diamond from the Ask Aan island, is flat and matte everywhere; there is no glossy object on the site. Rejected and gone: soft clay (`ClayBlock`, the clay mascot shading, the clay CTA), the glossy three.js Aan, the City Nocturne line-art scenes and the soft isometric "circuit" drawings. None of them return.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Three colour layers: the page ground follows the theme, brand stages stay one colour in both themes, product mockups follow the theme with the app's own tokens.
- Two registers on one palette: studio (Inter 500, gradient cards, black worlds, pinned GSAP scenes) on the homepage and Signals; screen (Inter 800, `Screen`, `ProductFrame`, `LightTrails`, travelling Aan) on the other product pages.
- The site's own hues are periwinkle (230) and coral (355); the app's green, red and amber appear only inside product mockups.
- Real app elements, never whole pages, always labelled "Sample data" or "Illustrative".
- Lenis smooth scroll under GSAP ScrollTrigger timelines that are pinned and scrubbed; transforms and opacity only while scrolling; the chrome reads its tone on a 90ms throttle, never per frame.
- Aan is flat, matte and limbless everywhere.
- IBM Plex Mono for indexes, clocks, figures and honest labels; Fraunces for written moments and for the single italic word in a Signals headline.

## Colors

Periwinkle is light, action and what Aan handled; coral is Aan or what is going wrong. Every colour belongs to one of three layers, and the layer decides how it behaves when the theme changes.

1. **Page ground** follows the theme (`--ground`, `--ground-ink`, `--ground-ink-2`, `--ground-line`, set on `.nocturne` in `app/styles/tokens.css`): white by day, `#0b0e14` by night. It is the homepage's ring stage and marquee, the gallery ground and lead, and the frame around the finale's open card.
2. **Brand stages** stay one colour in both themes: the hero card, the ring cards, the gallery panels and the finale card (blue-to-periwinkle gradients with coral glows), the black world inside Aan, the finale's dark space, and the whole Signals stage (black, because the halo needs it).
3. **Product mockups** follow the theme the way the app does, through the `--app-*` tokens taken from the revamp app (anarix-revamp-scope-2027 `frontend/src/index.css`): the app's light UI by day, its dark UI by night.

The screen register takes every role from the `.nocturne` scope (day values under `html[data-theme="light"] .nocturne`) and changes whole with the theme.

### Primary
- **Working Periwinkle** (`periwinkle-solid`, `--accent-solid`): the fill of the screen register's primary CTA and of solid UI buttons, and the user's question bubble in screen-register product UI. Deepens to `periwinkle-solid-hover-night` on hover.
- **Handled Periwinkle** (`periwinkle-night` / `periwinkle-day`, `--accent`): the light itself in the screen register. Light-trail strokes and pools, focus rings, link hover, index numerals, handled states. Day drops it to 50% lightness to hold contrast on pale sky.
- **Act Periwinkle** (`periwinkle-act`, `--act` on Signals): Signals' action colour, where Relay had lime. Fills the action pill (with Stage Ink text), live dots, progress fills and the halo's dominant fibre colour.
- **Lit** (`lit-night` / `lit-day`, `--lit`): a translucent periwinkle wash for floor glows.

### Secondary
- **Aan Coral** (`aan-coral`, `--risk`, `--coral`): Aan's body, the one risk trail, risk marks in screen-register UI, the warm glow at the foot of studio gradients, and the faint diamond ghost in the corner of Signals' close. The mascot's own gradient stops (`#f88a93`, `#f57780`, `#f05e6a`) belong to the mascot and are not tokens.
- **Risk Ink** (`risk-ink-night` / `risk-ink-day`, `--risk-ink`): coral as text, for costs and losses in screen-register figures.

### Tertiary
- **Studio Gradient** (`studio-deep` → `studio-mid` → `studio-bright`): the homepage hero card and the finale card, `linear-gradient(160deg, studio-deep, studio-mid 45%, studio-bright)` under a coral radial at the foot and a periwinkle one in the middle. The six ring cards and gallery panels each take a variant (deep blue for Signals and Advertising, coral-lit near-black for Profitability, deep violet-blue for Rules, near-black with a periwinkle corner for MCP, periwinkle into lilac with a coral foot for Ask Aan).
- **Signals Surface** (`--surface`): `linear-gradient(115deg, #f3828a, #c993d6 48%, #8a9cf6)` under a pale coral radial, the coral to periwinkle surface where Relay used lime.
- **Film Stamp Red** (`film-stamp-red`): the stamped words of the "Aan's debut" film, on its route only.

### App (product mockups only)
The `--app-*` set, in a day and a night value each, used only inside `components/app-ui`:
- **App Bg, Card, Raise** (`app-bg-*`, `app-card-*`, `app-raise-*`): the app's page, card and raised-control surfaces.
- **App Line** (`app-line-*`, plus `--app-line-2` for inner dividers): 1px card borders and rules.
- **App Ink, Ink 2, Muted, Faint** (`app-ink-*` … `app-faint-*`): the app's text steps, from titles to timestamps and the "Sample data" note.
- **App Primary** (`app-primary-*`, with `--app-primary-ink`, `--app-tint`, `--app-tint-line`, `--app-lilac`): the app's own periwinkle for selection, primary buttons, tints and chart series.
- **App Gain, Risk, Warn** (`app-gain-*`, `app-risk-*`, `app-warn-*`, each with a `-tint`): the app's green for gains and Done, red for money at risk and High priority, amber for warnings.
- **App Shadow** (`--app-shadow`): see Elevation.

### Neutral
- **Ground** (`ground-day` / `ground-night`, `--ground`) with **Ground Ink** and **Ground Ink 2** (`--ground-ink`, `--ground-ink-2`): the page ground and the type set directly on it (the marquee, the gallery lead, the explore button). `--ground-line` is defined (`hsl(228 20% 88%)` / white at 10%) but not yet used.
- **Sky** (`sky-night` / `sky-day`, `--sky`): the screen register's page ground; the body behind `.nocturne` matches it so overscroll never flashes.
- **Ink** and **Ink 2** (`ink-*`, `ink-2-*`): headlines and key phrases in Ink; statements, captions, notes and mono indexes in Ink 2. Signals sets its own steps on the black stage (`--ink` `hsl(228 20% 95%)`, `--ink-2` `hsl(226 14% 76%)`, `--ink-3` `hsl(226 10% 60%)` for mono labels, `--ink-4` `hsl(226 8% 46%)` for dim text).
- **Rule** (`rule-*`, `--rule`): the 1px hairline that groups ruled lists, proof figures and the permission ladder. On black and on brand stages, hairlines are white at 9% to 20%.
- **Card** and **Card Line** (`card-*`, `card-line-*`): the ProductFrame and screen-register product UI surface and its border.
- **Void** (`void`): the black of the world inside Aan's window and the finale's dark space.
- **Signals Black** (`signals-black`): the Signals stage, in both themes.
- **Stage Ink** (`stage-ink`): near-black text on white studio buttons and on the act pill.
- **Bubble** (`bubble-bg-*`, `bubble-ink-*`): Aan's speech bubble, inverted against the theme.
- **Video Ground** (`video-ground`): the near-black behind the film player and product video.
- **Chrome** (`chrome-*`, the `--c-*` set): the nav, Ask Aan island and footer only, ported from www.anarix.ai and switched by `data-theme`, and by `data-nav` / `data-island` from the tone system.

### Named Rules
**The Three Layers Rule.** Every colour is ground, stage or mockup. The ground follows the theme; a brand stage keeps its colours in both themes; a mockup follows the theme through `--app-*` and never takes a stage's colours. Nothing on a stage reads `--ground`, and nothing on the ground is a fixed white or black.

**The No Green Rule.** The site's own type, chrome, gradients, trails and halo never use green: positive is periwinkle, negative is coral. Green enters only inside a product mockup, as the app's own `--app-gain` for gains and Done, beside the app's red (`--app-risk`) for risk and amber (`--app-warn`) for warnings. Outside `components/app-ui` no hue between roughly 60 and 180 appears.

**The Two Hues Rule.** Outside product mockups, only periwinkle (230) and coral (355) carry hue. A studio surface may blend from one to the other, and the blend's middle stops are the violets between them (`#c993d6`, `#6a5fe0`); a violet never appears on its own as a flat fill, a text colour or a UI state.

**The Coral Is Aan's Rule.** On the site's own surfaces coral is Aan, something going wrong, or the warm glow at the foot of a studio gradient. It is never a button fill, a heading, a link or a state of success.

**The One Risk Trail Rule.** A set of light trails is all periwinkle, except that a screen about a risk may run exactly one trail coral (`risk` index on `LightTrails`), and only while that risk is live.

**The Scope Rule.** In the screen register, colours come from the `.nocturne` roles (`--sky`, `--ink`, `--ink-2`, `--accent`, `--accent-solid`, `--risk`, `--risk-ink`, `--rule`, `--card`, `--card-line`, `--bubble-*`) and theme is the whole page at a different hour. In the studio register, ground, stage and mockup colours come from `--ground-*`, the stage's own fixed values and `--app-*`.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Inter (with system-ui, sans-serif), via `--font-display`
**Body Font:** Inter, via `--font-body`
**Label/Mono Font:** IBM Plex Mono (with ui-monospace), via `--font-mono`
**Editorial Font:** Fraunces (with Georgia, serif), via `--font-editorial`

**Character:** Swiss in two weights. The screen register shouts in Inter 800 with tight tracking; the studio register speaks in Inter 500, large and tight, with a second line dropped to white at 50–60%. Plex Mono does the counting and labelling in small sizes; Fraunces appears where a piece of writing should feel written, and as one italic word in a Signals headline. Mockups use the app's own Inter sizes, all in em.

### Hierarchy
- **Display Name** (Inter 800, 0.9, -0.055em): a product's own name in its `NameHero`, the largest type on its page. Size is set per hero layout, from `clamp(3.5rem, min(9vw, 13svh), 9rem)` up to `clamp(5rem, min(14vw, 24svh), 15rem)`.
- **Headline** (Inter 800, `--fs-h2`, 0.98, -0.04em, balanced): every screen heading in the screen register (`.t-h2`).
- **Studio Display** (Inter 500, 1.02, -0.045em): the Signals hero headline. The homepage hero title (`clamp(2.3rem, 3.7vw, 4.2rem)`, 1, -0.04em), the world title ("AI for good. / Built to earn your trust.", `clamp(2.4rem, 4.4vw, 4.6rem)`), the gallery lead (`clamp(2.4rem, 4vw, 4.2rem)`) and the finale close (`clamp(2.4rem, 4.6vw, 5rem)`) are the same face and weight at their own sizes, each with its second line at white 50–60% or Ground Ink 2.
- **Studio Headline** (Inter 500, 1.08, -0.04em): Signals section headings. Gallery panel headlines are Inter 500 `clamp(1.7rem, 2.6vw, 2.6rem)` at 1.06.
- **Marquee** (Inter 500, `clamp(3.4rem, 7.2vw, 8.5rem)`, -0.045em, Ground Ink): the giant words that slide behind the product ring, separated by small outlined circles. The gallery's product names use the same weight at `clamp(2.6rem, 5.2vw, 5.6rem)` in white.
- **Slogan** (Inter 600, 1.1, -0.015em, Ink 2): the line under a product name.
- **Figure** (Inter 800, 1, -0.04em, tabular): proof figures in a ruled row. Signals' figures are Inter 500, tabular, `clamp(2.8rem, 4.4vw, 3.8rem)`, counted up once. The homepage hero's stat strip sets its values in Inter 600 1.25rem.
- **Editorial** (Fraunces 400, 1.5rem, 1.1, -0.01em): the morning brief title and the letter on the Aan page. In Signals, Fraunces 400 italic sets one word or phrase inside an Inter 500 headline.
- **Statement** (Inter 400, `--fs-statement`, 1.5, max 42 to 44ch, Ink 2): the paragraph under a screen-register headline, key phrases in `<b>` at 500 and full Ink. Studio ledes are 0.9375rem at 1.55–1.6, white at 80–86% or Ground Ink 2.
- **Point title / text** (Inter 600 15px / 400 14px, 1.45, Ink 2): ruled points. The world's three principles are Inter 600 1rem over 0.8125rem lines.
- **App UI** (Inter, `--app-fs`, default 13px, 1.35, tabular): every mockup. Its parts are em steps of that base (titles 1.04em/600, meta 0.85em, pills 0.77em/600–700), so a mockup is scaled by setting `--app-fs` on its container (11–11.5px for floating pieces, `calc(var(--cw) * 0.031)` on ring cards, `clamp(10px, 1.42svh, 12.5px)` on gallery panels).
- **Index** (Plex Mono 500, 0.625–0.75rem, 0.06em, uppercase where it labels): sequence numerals ("01 / 06" on the ring cards, "01" beside a gallery name, the three start steps) and the small "The platform" label over the hero index.
- **Frame note** (Plex Mono 500, 0.6875rem; 0.72em inside mockups): the honest label in a ProductFrame's pill, at the foot of every app-ui card, and Signals' mono labels (`0.6875rem/1.4`, `--ink-3`).

### Named Rules
**The Weight Per Register Rule.** Screen-register headlines are Inter 800 between -0.04em and -0.055em, line-height under 1, fluid by both width and height (`min(vw, svh)`). Studio-register headlines are Inter 500 between -0.04em and -0.045em, line-height 1 to 1.08. Never mix the two weights in one section.

**The Grey Prose, Ink Phrase Rule.** Prose is the quieter ink; only the phrases that carry the argument switch to full ink at 500. In studio headlines the same move runs the other way: the second line drops to white at 50–60%. Emphasis is contrast, not colour or size.

**The Written Word Rule.** Fraunces is for writing that reads as written by someone (the morning brief, Aan's letter) and, in Signals only, for one italic word per headline. It never sets a whole headline, a figure or UI.

**The Mono Counts Rule.** Plex Mono sets numbers that count, time or order something, and the sample-data notes. Every figure that changes uses tabular numerals.

**The Em Scale Rule.** A mockup is sized by `--app-fs` and everything inside it in em; it is never scaled with a CSS or GSAP `scale` to fit. Motion may scale a piece in flight (the hero pieces flying into the centre card), never to size it.

### Motion
**Smooth scroll.** `SmoothScroll` (in the site layout) runs Lenis (`lerp 0.085`, touch multiplier 1.4) from the GSAP ticker with lag smoothing off, and ScrollTrigger updates from Lenis, so scrubbed timelines read one smooth value per frame. Hash links glide via Lenis. Reduced motion keeps the browser's own scrolling.

**Scroll scenes.** Each scene is one GSAP timeline in `gsap.matchMedia`, pinned and scrubbed (`scrub: 1`, `invalidateOnRefresh`, `anticipatePin`), with GSAP owning every transform it moves, centring included. The Intro is a hand-off, not a swap: the ring's own front card is under the hero from the start; the hero copy lifts away, the five floating pieces fly into the centre card (scale 0.3, fading), the frame tilts and closes onto that card by `clip-path`, the side cards rise in from depth (scale 0.84 to 1) while it closes, the hero fades off the front card at rest, and the ring starts turning (60° per card) before the collapse ends. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the scene sets `data-static="true"` and shows its end state laid out in flow: the hero, the six cards and the world one after another; the gallery row scrolls sideways by hand; the close framed by the ground without fragments.

**Curves and durations.** `--ease-arrive` `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for what arrives or settles; `--ease-travel` `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` for Aan's flights and trail pulses; `--ease-spring` `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` for Aan's acts; Signals uses `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` and GSAP's `power` eases. Durations: micro 160ms, UI 320ms, reveal 700ms, flight 600ms, stagger 90ms. `Reveal` is the blur-rise (opacity 0, 16px down, 4px blur) and runs only when script is running.

**The Smooth Scroll Rule.** Nothing re-renders React per scroll frame: scroll scenes write transforms and opacity through GSAP, the halo sets shader uniforms, the chrome tone reads at most every 90ms, and layout is measured on ScrollTrigger refresh only (`invalidateOnRefresh`, function-based values, the halo's `measure` on `refresh`). WebGL draws only while it can be seen (the sphere is switched by its ScrollTrigger, the halo stops when the tab is hidden). Reduced motion shows each scene's end state and leaves Lenis off.

**The Transform And Opacity Rule.** Animate transform and opacity. The exceptions the build carries are the blur-rise reveals, the trails' stroke draw, colour and glow transitions on hover and state, Aan's cube and bar morphs, the Intro's hero frame collapsing onto its centre card by `clip-path`, and the `data-tone` attribute sets; add no more.

**The Depth Drift Rule.** Parallax exists only inside the studio register, as depth: the hero's floating pieces lean with the pointer by their depth while the Intro is at rest, each gallery panel's mock drifts 10% each way against its frame, and the finale's fragments travel at speeds set by their depth. The screen register has none.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put every colour in its layer: page ground from `--ground-*`, brand stages fixed in both themes, product mockups from `--app-*`.
- **Do** keep periwinkle and coral as the site's only hues; let studio gradients blend between them, and leave the app's green, red and amber inside mockups.
- **Do** declare `data-tone` on every studio section and set it from the timeline when a pinned scene changes what sits under the chrome.
- **Do** take every screen-register colour from the `.nocturne` roles so night and day both work.
- **Do** set screen-register headlines in Inter 800 (`.t-h2`, the name sizes) and studio headlines in Inter 500, and keep Fraunces for the morning brief, Aan's letter and one italic word per Signals headline.
- **Do** build mockups from single app-ui elements, sized by `--app-fs`, with neutral sample names and a "Sample data" or "Illustrative" note.
- **Do** give the homepage the whole platform (Signals, Profitability, Advertising, Rules, MCP, Ask Aan), never a page about Aan alone.
- **Do** keep proof figures to $1.2B GMV, 500+ brands and 12.8% TACoS.
- **Do** name marketplaces and apps (Amazon, Walmart, Shopify, Slack, Claude) as text.
- **Do** build scroll scenes as pinned, scrubbed GSAP timelines over Lenis, moving transforms and opacity, measuring on refresh only, and mark them `data-pin` so `fit-check.js` checks them through the pin; run QA in both themes.
- **Do** pause WebGL when it cannot be seen and load three.js only when the component mounts.
- **Do** design the reduced-motion state on purpose: each scene's end state shown in flow, nothing travelling, no Lenis.
- **Do** keep the nav, the Ask Aan island and the footer at parity with www.anarix.ai.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use green, teal or any hue outside periwinkle (230) and coral (355) on the site's own type, chrome, gradients, trails or halo, or a flat violet fill on its own.
- **Don't** paint a brand stage with `--ground`, or let a mockup keep dark colours in the light theme.
- **Don't** use coral for buttons, headings, links or success states.
- **Don't** recreate a whole app page as a mockup, invent customers, or show an unlabelled number.
- **Don't** scale a mockup with a transform to make it fit; change `--app-fs`.
- **Don't** give Aan arms, legs, hands or a face beyond its two eyes, or restyle it outside the island's `AanMascot` and the ring card's white diamond.
- **Don't** bring back soft clay, a glossy or clearcoated Aan, City Nocturne line-art scenes or the soft isometric drawings.
- **Don't** set React state from scroll, animate layout properties while scrolling, measure the DOM on a scroll frame, or check the chrome tone per frame.
- **Don't** invent testimonials, logos or prices, or draw marketplace or app logos.
- **Don't** let a `fit="screen"` section or a pinned scene grow past one viewport above 620px of height.
- **Don't** add hover lifts, hard offset shadows, glossy surfaces or gradient text.
- **Don't** add parallax outside the studio register, or a second pinned story to a screen-register page.
- **Don't** bring the film's stamp red, Bodoni or grain onto site pages.
